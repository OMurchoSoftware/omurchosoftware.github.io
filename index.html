Featured program

Heritage Tracker

A record keeper for genealogists who want proof, not guesswork.

  • Keep each ancestor's birth, marriage, death and census records together, with a picture of the actual document.
  • A checklist shows at a glance which records are missing, which still need an image, and which never existed for that time and place.
  • Pedigree charts, plus "How Are We Related?" and "Descended From" picture charts large enough to show on a projector at a family reunion.
  • Bring in your existing family tree from RootsMagic, even trees with tens of thousands of people.
  • Not sure which parents are right? Keep both possible lines until one is proven.

The Heritage family

Photo tools that work alongside Heritage Tracker

Heritage Photo Match

Put names to the faces in old family photos.

  • Crop each face from a group photo into a searchable face library.
  • Facial recognition suggests possible matches for unidentified faces, right on your own computer.
  • Record your best estimate of when a photo was taken, and why.
  • Rename and tag your original photos with names and years, so Windows search can find them.

Heritage Photo Scanner

Scan a whole stack of old prints in a few passes.

  • Lay several photos on the scanner glass; each one is found, straightened and saved as its own picture.
  • Give every photo its own name before saving.
  • 600 dpi for most prints, 1200 dpi or more for small originals.
  • Works with most flatbed scanners that have a Windows driver.
